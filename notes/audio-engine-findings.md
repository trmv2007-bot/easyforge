# Audio Engine Features: World-Class Engines vs. EasyForge (No-Code Godot)

Research basis: Godot 4.7 official docs (AudioStreamPlayer2D/3D, AudioStreamWAV/Randomizer, buses, effects, Area3D reverb_bus_amount), Unity 6 docs (AudioSource, AudioMixer, snapshots, AudioLowPassFilter), Unreal 5 docs (Sound Attenuation, MetaSounds, plugin spatialization), FMOD/Wwise docs and community tutorials, Steam Audio docs, rhythm-game and adaptive-music literature.

Constraint lens: Godot 4.7 official (no fork), deterministic bricks-to-GDScript compiler, no raw user code, local standalone, procedural assets only, 16GB RAM / RTX 3050 (comfortable for audio workloads).

| # | Feature | One-liner | Effort | Beginner impact | Constraint fit |
|---|---------|-----------|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| 1 | 2D/3D spatial audio (panning + distance falloff) | Pan and attenuate sounds by position; Godot ships AudioStreamPlayer2D/3D with attenuation models, unit_size, max_distance; Unreal/Unity have equivalent source setups | S (2D) / M (3D full params) | Very high — instant "game feel" | Full: engine-native, compiles to deterministic GDScript |
| 2 | HRTF / binaural spatialization | Head-related filtering for directional headphones; Unity/Unreal need plugins (Oculus Audio, Steam Audio); Godot has no built-in HRTF | L | Medium (needs headphones to notice) | Partial: needs GDExtension; poor fit for "no fork" unless deferred |
| 3 | Doppler effect | Pitch shift on moving sources; Godot AudioStreamPlayer3D has doppler_tracking enum, on by default | S | Medium | Full: property exposure only |
| 4 | Audio buses + mixing (volume/mute routing) | Route sounds to named buses (Music/SFX/UI) with per-bus volume/mute; Godot buses support any number + chained effects; Unity AudioMixer is the analog | M | Very high — slider-level beginner win, prerequisite for everything below | Full: AudioServer bus API is deterministic |
| 5 | DSP effects (reverb, chorus, delay, EQ, compressor, distortion) | Per-bus effect chains; Godot ships ~10 effects incl. reverb, chorus, EQ bands, compressor, distortion, limiter; Unity/Unreal similar via mixer/filter chains | M (wiring) / L (exposing all params) | High | Full: bus effect API; big compiler surface if over-exposed — keep presets |
| 6 | Reverb zones (Area-based) | Volume that changes reverb as you enter a room/cave; Godot Area3D has reverb_bus_amount + reverb bus routing natively; Unity/Unreal need manual zones | M | High | Full: Area3D + bus pair exposed as a "Reverb Zone" brick |
| 7 | Occlusion / obstruction | Muffle sounds behind walls via raycast + low-pass filter; Unity ships AudioLowPassFilter on source/listener; Godot has no built-in — implement via Area/Physics raycast in generated GDScript | M | Medium-high | Full-feasible: raycast is deterministic; authored as one brick |
| 8 | Physics-based sound propagation (Steam Audio etc.) | Realistic reflections/baking; Steam Audio has a community Godot GDExtension (Nexus Resonance) | L | Low for beginners (CPU bake cost, subtlety) | Poor: GDExtension + baking pipeline; defer |
| 9 | Audio pooling + voice limits | Cap simultaneous sounds to avoid noise/CPU spikes; Godot max_polyphony on AudioStreamPlayer, AudioStreamPolyphonic; FMOD/Wwise have full voice management | M | High (prevents the "20 enemies = noise" beginner trap) | Full: compiler can pool deterministically |
| 10 | Streaming vs preloaded | Stream long music from disk, preload short SFX; Godot AudioStream import has streamed flag / playbacks; Unity "Streaming" load type | S (import setting) | Low-medium (perf nicety) | Full: import flag in compiled project |
| 11 | Pitch/volume randomization | Random pick + pitch_scale variance to avoid the "same footstep" fatigue; Godot AudioStreamRandomizer does pool+pitch+volume natively; Unity audio-random-container via middleware | S | Very high — classic beginner-quality jump | Full: one brick wrapping AudioStreamRandomizer |
| 12 | Loop points | Seamless music/SFX loops; Godot AudioStreamWAV loop_mode + loop_begin/loop_end; FMOD/Wwise loop regions in the tool | S | High (needed for BGM and ambient loops) | Full: WAV metadata + importer flag |
| 13 | Procedural audio synthesis | Generate audio buffers at runtime; Godot AudioStreamGenerator (C++ or GDScript fill); engines use it for synth SFX and reactive music | M | Medium (mostly behind-the-scenes for EasyForge's synth SFX — already have it) | Full: already using synthesized WAVs; could extend at compile-time |
| 14 | Adaptive music — layers (vertical remixing) | Stems/layers fade in/out by game state (combat/explore); FMOD/Wwise core feature; Godot needs manual multi-player + tween fades | L | High for polish, high effort | Partial: doable with generated GDScript, but a big compiler feature |
| 15 | Adaptive music — stingers + transitions | One-shot accents on events (level-up jingle) and crossfades/horizontal resequencing between cues; middleware staples | M | High | Full for stingers (trigger brick); crossfade = S bus tween |
| 16 | Audio-driven gameplay / rhythm sync | Sync visuals/logic to beat; Godot: pitch_scale speed change (NOT time_scale), AudioServer.get_time_since_last_mix() latency comp, BPM conductor patterns; asset lib has RhythmNotifier | M | Medium (rhythm-genre only) | Full: deterministic timing via AudioServer clock |
| 17 | Surface-based footsteps | Material/raycast picks per-surface sound set (grass/stone/water); Unity/Unreal via physical materials; Godot via raycast + tilemap/collision metadata | M | Medium-high | Full-feasible: physics trace in generated GDScript |
| 18 | Occlusion via area zones (ambient/underwater muffling) | Entering water/cave swaps an ambient bed + low-pass; combo of reverb zones + ducking | M | Medium | Full: Area3D + bus effects |
| 19 | Middleware integration (FMOD/Wwise for Godot) | Bring full adaptive-music tooling into Godot; community GDExtensions exist (fmod-for-godot, wwise-godot-integration) but fmod-for-godot is unmaintained; both are binary add-ons | L | Low (adds external tool) | Poor: violates local-standalone/no-extra-tooling spirit; in-house only |

| 21 | Ducker / sidechain (music dips when SFX plays) | Auto-lower music bus when a voice/SFX bus fires; Unity mixer ducking, FMOD/Wwise built-in; Godot needs manual bus-volume tween | M | Medium | Full: simple generated tween logic |

## Top 5 Ranked (for EasyForge's next audio milestone)

1. **Audio buses + mixing (with volume sliders + DSP presets)** — S/M, very high impact, fully engine-native and deterministic; the foundation every other feature (reverb zones, ducking, transitions) builds on. Biggest beginner win per line of generated code.
2. **Pitch/volume randomization (AudioStreamRandomizer)** — S, very high impact, one-property brick; kills the "repetitive SFX" amateur tell instantly. Best effort-to-impact ratio on the board.
3. **3D spatial audio (pan + distance falloff + doppler)** — S/M, very high impact, pure property exposure on AudioStreamPlayer2D/3D; makes games feel "real" with zero physics work. HRTF/deep 3D deferred as the poor-constraint-fit tail.
4. **Loop points + seamless BGM/ambient loops** — S, high impact, just WAV import flags; without it there is no music system at all, and it's the cheapest possible music enabler.
5. **Reverb zones + ducking (Area3D reverb_bus_amount + music-dip)** — M, high impact, both compile from Area3D triggers + bus volume tweens; turns flat mixes into "designed" audio and reuses the bus infrastructure from #1.

Runner-up (next wave): adaptive music stingers (#15, M) and surface footsteps (#17, M) — both feasible and popular, but they need the bus + pooling foundation first.
